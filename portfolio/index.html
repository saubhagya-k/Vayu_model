Case study

Why a gas + electric hybrid?

This started as a Suzuki futuristic-car assignment. Instead of choosing between a full EV and a fuel car, I asked which powertrain fits how Indian families actually drive.

01 · PROBLEM

EVs and CNG each solve half the problem

Full EVs are cheap to run but bring range anxiety, thin charging coverage outside big cities and expensive batteries. CNG is cheap per km and widely available, but today's CNG cars give up boot space and performance.

02 · INSIGHT

Most trips are short. A few are long.

Daily commutes fit easily within a small battery. Highway trips and hill drives are occasional, but they are the reason people don't buy EVs. One car should handle both without compromise.

03 · SOLUTION

Electric first, gas as a safety net

VAYU drives on its battery by default. A small gas-flex engine running on CNG or LPG takes over on long trips and recharges the battery on the move. The concept also builds on Suzuki's existing CNG expertise as the brand moves into EVs.

How it works

Four drive modes, chosen automatically

The car picks the most efficient energy source for each moment. Click a mode to see it in the 3D model and on the driver display.

Design language

Key design decisions

  • 01
    Flat floor, full bootThe battery sits between the axles and the gas tanks are split into two slim under-floor cylinders, so there's no big tank in the boot.
  • 02
    Mode-aware "energy line"A light strip along the side glows in the colour of the active mode. Blue tells pedestrians that a silent EV is nearby, which helps safety and communication.
  • 03
    Floating roof & full-width light barA dark glass canopy with body-colour blades and one continuous LED signature front and rear give a clean, futuristic and aerodynamic look.
  • 04
    Honest HMIThe driver display shows exactly where the energy is coming from and what each km costs, so efficient driving becomes visible and rewarding.
Process

From assignment to interactive prototype

I used AI tools throughout to research faster, explore more options and turn the concept into something people can interact with, not just a static render.

01

Research

Studied Indian driving patterns, CNG and EV ownership pain points, and the Suzuki brand direction.

02

Concept & packaging

Defined the hybrid architecture and placed the battery, tanks, engine and motor for a flat floor and full boot.

03

3D modelling

Built the car as a real-time WebGL model in Three.js, with an X-ray view and an animated energy flow.

04

HMI & interaction

Designed the driver display and the mode interactions, all linked to the 3D model in real time.
